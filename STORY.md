## Character Profiles (Image Gallery)

### Cover Art
*By Altiz Studio*

### Azrael
*Human Drow Fighter*

Servant of Evanstaul, he seeks to prove himself and to obey his master's last command to rid the world of the elvish invaders.

### Wren
*Human Master Paladin*

Servant of the High Priest, a noble fighter whose mission is to find evil where ever it hides and destroy it for the sake of all that is good.

### Lucette
*Halfling Swordmaster*

Feeling helpless at the death of her parents, she chose to empower herself by training and travelling with Evalun so that she can protect everyone she loves.

### Thomas
*Human Paladin Apprentice*

A devoted apprentice who sought to become a paladin after his village was saved from Grisgar. He has proven to be the most skilled for his young age.

### Chai
*Human Royal Princess*

The Princess of Duardain who was captured by Oberat only to return to a new catastrophe of the kingdom being taken over by Faust and his elvish mercenaries.

### Puck
*Elf Spellmaster*

This mischevious spellmaster is the successor to Oberat and is adept at casting vicious spells with little effort.  He is able to teleport behind his enemies.

### Dor
*Elf Champion*

A strong brute force elf who can thrash any opponent with his crushing attacks.  His blatant temper has made him fall out of favor with Faust.

### Miwa
*Elf Enchantress*

A beautiful but deadly elvish enchantress who can crush an entire army with her spellcasting abilities.  She can cause a magic torrent to fall on her enemies.

### Seeda
*Elf Swordmaster*

A cold and emotionless elvish mercenary whose lightning quick swordplay cannot be matched by any normal humans.  He always prefers to be in solitude.

### Mad Leoric
*Human Bounty Hunter*

Leoric allied with Stradlater to search for Faust in the other world but was betrayed and left to die.  He returned on his own, seeking murderous revenge.

### Stradlater
*Human Paladin Seer*

A trusted advisor to Faust, using his seer powers, he foretold of the bleak future that awaits the entire human race and decided to ally to the side of the elves.

### Faust
*Human Paladin King*

A traitor to the kingdom sent to exile in a remote island.  He brought back with him evil elvish mercenaries from the other world. He intends to become King.

### Dreadlord
*Human Enchanted Knight*

In a world hopeless against the onslaught of magic attacks, these huge knights possess both physical and magical powers used for good and evil.

### Evalun
*Human Drow Fighter*

The daughter of Evanstaul, she seeks to avenge her father's death at the hands of the elves.  Her ultimate goal is to destroy the grand leader of the elves.

### Evanstaul
*Human Drow Lord*

From an ancient race of secretive gothic humans.  No one knows his intentions, or which side he is on. Rumored having incredible powers and considered unkillable.

## Prologue

**Return of Faust**

A high paladin who lacked virtue was banished years ago to an
island of outcasts.  His traitorous plan to overthrow the
king of Duardain had failed.  Princess Chai, showing him mercy,
had stayed his execution, but it was not long until there were
reports of strange happenings on that island.  Those who set
sail to investigate had disappeared in the ocean's peril, including
a large party of paladins and knights.  There was no doubt to the
king that Faust was responsible for these acts.

So the king sent Stradlater, a master paladin and seer, trained
in both light magic from the High Priest and dark arts from the Drow.
Along with him a ravenous bounty hunter named Leoric, with unmatched
marksmanship.  Their secret mission was to find Faust and kill him
outright using stealth and precision.  It did not go according to
their plans, far from it.

Azrael had used all his might to bring down the elf Oberat, while
Wren and his paladins had layed the final blow with a ring of fire.
Princess Chai was saved.  But Oberat was only the beginning of
something that humans had never faced before.

The princess returned to her castle to find a familiar face ...
